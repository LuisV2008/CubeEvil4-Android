# CubeEvil4 v1.1.7

> Keep a copy of your saves (app menu → Management → Export Player Data (.zip)).

Changes since v1.1.6.

## New

- Quick weapon switch (Controls → Assists, off by default): change weapons without opening the case, in the case's order: D-pad left/right (not while aiming), keys 1–9 or the mouse wheel, a tap or swipe on the life gauge, or your own keys for "Next Weapon" / "Previous Weapon" in the button mapping; the new weapon's name shows briefly above the gauge. Grenades only with "Grenades in the cycle".

## Fixed

- Move and aim: a crash when walking with the aim after switching weapons (e.g. back to the handgun after the sniper rifle) or after a room was loaded again.
- Minimap: a crash when the ending movie started while treasures and items were shown on the minimap.
- Minimap: it no longer reads the room while the case, the merchant's shop or the files are open (a possible crash there with Treasures and items on).
- Graphics (OpenGL ES): when the graphics driver runs out of memory, the game now lets go of textures and shaders and notes it in the error log, instead of carrying on until it crashes (a possible cause of crashes in the case and at the merchant on low-end GPUs).

**Found a bug?** Keep "Record the error log" on (app menu → Management → Error log, on by default) and send
the report right away, without closing the game first: app menu (SELECT) → Management → **Send report**,
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AB3WVBqD5G) or as an issue here.
